# Shelas A L — Machine Learning Engineer & Systems Developer Portfolio

An artistic, minimalist developer portfolio crafted for **Shelas A L** (Machine Learning Engineer & B.Tech CSE AI/ML student at Lovely Professional University).

Engineered with **pure semantic HTML5, modern CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript** (zero external build tools or npm dependencies), featuring an **artistic warm beige & roasted espresso brown light theme** alongside an **atmospheric warm obsidian dark theme**, and highlighting two primary technical case studies: **Credit Risk Prediction System** and **PixelBridge**.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy & Features

- **Artistic Minimalism**: Clean, editorial typography pairing **Inter** and **Newsreader** serif italics with **JetBrains Mono** for technical parameter tables.
- **Light Theme**: Warm linen beige (`#f7f4ee`), oat surface cards (`#ffffff`), warm sandstone borders, and roasted espresso brown text (`#231c17`) with terracotta/saddle brown accents (`#8c5835`).
- **Dark Theme**: Atmospheric warm basalt obsidian (`#0f0e0d`), dark carbon cards (`#171614`), and warm cream text (`#f4eee7`) with amber bronze accents (`#d49b6a`).
- **Case Study 1 (Credit Risk AI)**: Random Forest, Scikit-learn, SHAP explainable AI, Joblib serialization, and Gradio interactive interface.
- **Case Study 2 (PixelBridge)**: High-throughput USB media synchronization via ADB, zero-byte laptop storage consumption, single-file ephemeral buffer, and EXIF timestamp preservation.
- **Verified Credentials**: Infosys Springboard Introduction to AI certification with an interactive lightbox inspection modal.
